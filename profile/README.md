# EduSine

EduSine gives schools, madrasahs, colleges and coaching centres in Bangladesh a free website and the school software to run them. Fees, attendance, exams and results are kept in one place, in Bangla and English.

## What it does

- Free school website
- Fees, with online payment through bKash and Nagad
- Attendance
- Exams, GPA results and marksheets
- SMS to guardians
- Admissions
- Student and guardian portals
- Bangla and English
